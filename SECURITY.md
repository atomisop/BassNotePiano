# Security

Thanks for helping keep Bass Note Piano and its students safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report it privately. Do not open a public issue or post it anywhere.

- Email: **security@REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-ADDRESS** (placeholder, the owner will fill this in)
- Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and what you think the impact is.
- We aim to reply within a few days and will tell you when it is fixed.

## In scope

- The website and app at bassnotepiano.com (sign-in, booking, payments, messages, admin, lesson files)
- Access to another person's data, accounts, lessons or payments
- Ways to get paid features or classes without paying
- Problems in this repository's code or its deployment settings

## Out of scope

- Denial of service, spam or automated scanning that loads the site heavily
- Social engineering of students or staff
- Issues that need a device or account you already control and expose only your own data
- Missing best-practice headers with no real impact

## Please do not

- Test against real student accounts or real payments. Make your own test account, and stop at the first sign of other people's data.
- Access, change or keep anyone else's data.

We will not take action against anyone who reports in good faith and follows these rules.
