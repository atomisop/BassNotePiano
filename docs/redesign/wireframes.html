Navigation: recommendation

Top bar on computers, bottom tab bar on phones and tablets.
  • Computer (1024px and wider): logo, then Home · Online Lessons · Friends · Messages · Settings in one row, then bell and your avatar (Profile) on the right. The floating dock goes away on computers.
  • Phone and tablet: the same five sections in a native-style bottom tab bar (like today's dock, but full width and pinned).
  • Why not a left sidebar: the lesson page already gets its own left column (the outline). A sidebar plus an outline would squeeze the lesson and piano, which should be as large as possible.
  • The onboarding tour's pointers move with the menu items, so the tour keeps working.

Decisions I need from you

  • Navigation: top bar on computers plus bottom tab bar on phones and tablets (recommended), or keep today's floating dock everywhere.
  • Notes and Metronome (dashed icons) do not exist in the app today. Adding them is new behavior, not just a new look. The other tools (Key, Scale chart, Chords, Files) already exist and only move. Build Notes and Metronome now, later, or never?
  • Lesson page merge: today the lesson list and the lesson itself are two separate views (you open a lesson, then go "back to sections"). The new design shows the outline and the lesson side by side. Same lessons, same completion rules, just one screen. OK?
  • Home tabs: "In progress / Completed / Badges" filter the same scale list and badge grid you have today. No new data.
