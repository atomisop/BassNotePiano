# Premium audit, 2026-10-30

Every screen was captured whole at 1440 and 390 wide, as a student (16 screens), the owner (19 admin screens) and a visitor (9 public
screens), and read for what keeps the app from feeling premium. Ranked from the change that lifts the feel most to the least.

| # | Finding | Where | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home was nine loud cards of nine colors; nothing stood out | Home | **Done**: one hero (the roadmap picture carrying today's step) and quiet rows with hairlines, colour only on the hero and the small icons |
| 2 | The top pop-up (phone number, "Time to practice") shows on almost every screen and covers the page title or greeting | All signed-in screens | **Needs the owner's call**: keep it on Home only, or turn the phone and practice asks into a row on Home instead of a pop-up |
| 3 | Empty screens were a bare sentence in a box ("No classes today.", "Nothing pending review.") | Admin Today, Payments, Applicants | **Done**: one designed empty state (dashed outline, warm icon, what to do next) |
| 4 | Boxes inside boxes | Lessons ("Your classes"), parts of Settings and the admin plans | **Done for Lessons** (class rows are plain rows with hairlines); Settings and admin plan cards left as they are |
| 5 | Full-width action bars inside cards look heavy | Admin > Billing > Plans | **Done**: Resend bill and Cancel plan are compact buttons at the end of each plan, Cancel quiet and red |
| 6 | The dock looked puffed up (3D light and shade) | Every signed-in screen | **Done**: a flat matte pill with a fine grain and a hairline, rounded like before |
| 7 | Narrow single columns on a wide screen leave most of the computer screen empty | About, Friends, Mail, Parent, Admin Today, Support | Open: give each a second column (details or a preview pane) from lg |
| 8 | The finger-exercise keyboard is flat grey blocks | Practice | Open: draw real keys (white and black, the pressed key in the accent) |
| 9 | Admin lists mix many small orange pills and badges | Admin Payments, Plans, Accounts | Open: one status style (dot + word), orange only for "needs you" |
| 10 | The marketing nav is six bordered pills | Public pages | Open: plain text links, with only "Get started" as a button |
