# UI and UX audit ledger

Filled in during each full audit, as `docs/UI-AUDIT-RULE.md` requires. One table per area. An audit is finished only when every skill in
the rule has a row (run or N/A with a reason) and every screen in its coverage list has been checked at the required widths and themes.

## Area 1: Admin (review pass, 2026-11-28)

**How it was checked.** Local build of `main` at `6fc41ff` plus the audit tools, Postgres seeded by `scripts/audit/seed-audit.mjs` (14 students in
every state: paid, late, waiting for first payment, paused, scholarship 50% and 100%, trial, complimentary, canceled; bills, classes this week,
bank emails, contacts, chats). `scripts/audit/crawl-shots.mjs` followed every link under `/admin` signed in as the owner: **53 pages and views**,
each shot at 1280, 768 and 390 in the dark theme and 1280 and 390 in the light theme (265 screenshots, full length), with axe (WCAG 2.2 AA),
overflow and counts of orange elements, buttons and small targets per page (`docs/ui-audit/admin/manifest.json`). A handful of shots are kept
in `docs/ui-audit/evidence/`; the full set is regenerated by the script and is not committed (46 MB).

**Not checked yet (rule 2):** 320px spot checks, loading and error states (a fault would have to be injected), keyboard pass, dialogs opened from
the student page (Set up plan, Plan settings, Book a class, Weekly time, Group, Mark paid, Fix payment), the owner with an empty studio.

**Seed artifacts, not app bugs:** "2 of 1 bills paid" on Liam (the seed gave a 1 Month plan two paid bills), classes shown under the wrong
window name ("Sat, 4:00 PM ... Monday evening"), every scale showing "No lessons yet" (the seed adds no lessons).

### What already works

No horizontal overflow on any of the 53 pages at any width; axe is clean everywhere except A15; the payment review card has one clear focal
point (the amount) and one orange Approve; the People list is real rows with a hairline frame.
