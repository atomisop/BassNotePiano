# Full app audit, 2026-11-02

What was checked: `tsc`, lint and the unit suite on the current tree; a read-only sweep of the live Neon database; every public page
at 375, 768 and 1280 wide with screenshots and axe-core (WCAG 2.2 AA + best practice); 30 signed-in and admin screens at 375 wide
with axe-core and layout metrics (overflow, tap targets, input sizes, headings, unnamed buttons); 14 signed-in screens at 768 and 12 at
1280 (the rest of that sweep was stopped by hand to save time); the background jobs, polling, security headers, dependencies and data
retention. The UI checks ran against a clean copy of `main` (what is live), not the working tree, because another session was
editing the working tree at the same time.

Nothing in the code was changed. This document is the only new file.

## A. Bugs to fix first (any device)

1. **Homepage sends iPhones a 17 MB video.** `Hero.tsx` on main lists the 4K HEVC file on R2 (17,124,723 bytes) first and every
   Safari (all iPhones and iPads) picks it. The 1080p H.264 copy (2.3 MB) only goes to browsers that cannot play HEVC. Fix: phones and
   tablets get the 720p/1080p copy by a `media` query, the 4K file only from 1024px up, and `preload="none"` under Save-Data.
2. **Classes sign-up form zooms the page on iPhone.** `fieldClass()` in `ClassRegistrationForm.tsx` uses `text-small` (13px) for
   Full name, Email, Phone and the notes box. House rule is 16px minimum. One class change.
3. **"Log in" and "Get started" are blank pills until the session call answers** (`MarketingNav.tsx`, `loadingSession`
   placeholders). On Pricing and Classes, the two conversion pages, a phone on a slow connection shows empty ovals for seconds. Fix:
   pass the signed-in state from the server layout so the real buttons render on first paint, no placeholder.
4. **Streak "last active" is never written.** `recordPracticeActivity` in `gamification.ts` has no caller, so `User.lastActivityDate`
   is null for 13 of 14 live students and the admin student page says "Last active: Never" next to "Streak: 8 days". The accounts
   list also sorts by this field. Fix: read "last active" from `KnownDevice.lastSeenAt` (the Data page already does) and drop the
   dead field from the student page and the sort.
5. **Piano keys in the scale player are too small to tap.** `InstrumentStage.tsx` draws black keys 21px wide; axe flags 20 keys
   under the 24px minimum. On phones draw one octave at a time (keys at least 28px) with a swipe to the next octave.
6. **Scale player row runs off the phone screen.** A `flex items-center` row in the player reaches 422px on a 375px screen (the
   instrument/"Hear it in C" row). Let it wrap or scroll.
7. **Profile badge tooltips are hover-only and overflow.** `BadgeGrid.tsx` shows a badge's name and how to earn it only on hover
   (`group-hover`, `pointer-events-none`, fixed `w-44`), so on phones and iPads nobody can read them, and the box runs past the right
   edge. Fix: tap opens a small sheet with the badge text; no hover tooltip on touch.
8. **Cookie banner covers the hero, pricing and every signed-in screen, and nothing needs it.** `CookieConsentBanner.tsx`'s own
   comment says the only cookie is the sign-in cookie, which is strictly necessary, and no code calls `hasConsent()`. On a phone the
   banner covers 40% of the screen and the "Get started" button; it even shows inside the app after sign-in. Fix: remove it, or keep a
   one-line "necessary cookie only" note in the footer and Privacy page.
9. **Admin Overview "Payments" link is 18px tall**, Sent list name links 18px, homepage "Start" links 18px, footer links 18px: all
   under the 24px minimum, none carry the `tap` utility. Add `tap` (or make the whole card the link).
10. **Email typo at sign-up bounces real mail.** A live student typed `gnail.com`; the reserved-time email bounced and nobody was
    told. Fix: suggest the correction for the common domains at sign-up ("Did you mean gmail.com?"), and raise an owner alert when
    Resend reports a bounce for a student address.

## B. Phone (375px, and check 320px)

11. **Homepage "Four steps" section is a pinned horizontal scroller.** On a phone it is 3 screens of near-empty black with a "Keep
    scrolling" hint. Render the four steps as a plain vertical list under 768px; keep the timeline on computers.
12. **Instrument picker on phones**: Piano is a full card, Guitar and Bass are crushed into 75px strips with the label floating over
    a sliver of photo. Give the three equal cards, or a horizontal snap row of three.
13. **Headline contrast**: the muted half of "Tabs teach you one song. Not the language." (`text-cream/40`) and the dimmed words of
    the scrolling paragraph (opacity 0.55) fail contrast, 58 nodes on every width. Lift the muted colour to about 62% and the resting
    word opacity to 0.7.
14. **Top bar credit badge** (`CreditsBadge.tsx`) is under 36px and has no `tap` class; it is on every signed-in screen.
15. **Messages empty state has two H1s and a skipped heading level** (axe `heading-order`), as do the Payments, Today and Students
    empty states (`EmptyState.tsx` uses `h3` right after `h1`). Use `h2`, or `p` with the title style.
16. **Streak page uses 10px text** (7 nodes at `text-[0.625rem]`, the weekday letters). Minimum 12px; weekday letters can be 12px at
    reduced opacity.
17. **Admin Messages page** has 13px checkboxes and 20px switches; add `hit-slop` as the student switches already have.
18. **Settings and About render their content twice** (phone slide-over and desktop column), and several pages leave a hidden second
    `h1` in the DOM. Harmless to sighted users, doubles the DOM and confuses screen-reader heading lists; mark the hidden copy `inert`
    or render once.
19. **Lesson files**: 15 homepage images and 9 shop images have no width/height, so the page jumps as they load. Give each image its
    size (or `aspect-ratio`) so the phone layout does not shift.

## C. iPad (768px)

20. **Instrument picker at 768** becomes one wide Piano card plus two 115px vertical strips with rotated labels that depend on hover to
    expand; on an iPad there is no hover. Same fix as 12: equal cards under 1024px.
21. **Marketing nav at 768** has empty placeholder pills (see 3) and no "Log in" until the session resolves.
22. **Scale player at 768**: the dock sits over the instrument chips at the foot of the first screen; the player's bottom controls
    need the same `pb-32` clearance the page body has.
23. **Lessons at 768**: the day list and the month calendar show the same information side by side and the calendar is squeezed;
    stack them (calendar first, collapsible) under 1024px.

## D. PC (1280px)

24. **Streak page: the right column overlaps the left cards** at 1280 (the "0 of 10 minutes" line and the practice grid are cut off
    under the goals panel). The two-column grid needs `min-w-0` and a gap, or the goals panel should drop under the grid until xl.
25. **Mail and Billing history empty states** are a bare sentence in a box on a 1280px screen ("No mail yet.", "No payments yet.").
    Use the designed `EmptyState` the admin pages got (icon, what it is, what to do next).
26. **Single narrow column on wide screens**: About, Friends, Mail, Parent, Support (already listed in `docs/PREMIUM-AUDIT.md` #7).
    Add a second pane from lg: a preview or details column.
27. **Homepage screenshots in the "Everything in one place" section are an older app** (Ask AI pill in the top bar, "Your next step"
    cards, a 4-day streak). The real Home is now one hero and rows. Re-capture the three screenshots from the current app
    (`scripts/make-blur-placeholders.mjs` after replacing them) so the marketing shows what students actually get.

## E. Homepage and hero: design pass

The hero is a cinematic studio video with the brand name as the headline. It looks premium but says nothing a visitor needs:
the name is already in the logo 60px above it, the promise is in 17px grey below it, and the two buttons sit at the very bottom
where the cookie banner covers them. Proposed shape, keeping the video and the type:

- **H1 is the promise, not the name**: "Learn Ethiopian scales on piano, guitar or bass, live with a teacher." (two lines on a phone,
  one on a computer), with the brand as a small eyebrow above it. This also fixes SEO: today the page's only H1 is "Bass Note Piano".
- **One primary button** ("Start live classes") and a quiet text link ("See pricing"), both in the upper two thirds of the screen, so
  nothing covers them.
- **One line of proof under the buttons**: "4 live classes every 28 days · weekly time reserved · curriculum opens with your teacher".
  True, specific, and the thing the pricing page repeats.
- **Video weight**: 720p for phones, 1080p for tablets, 4K only on large screens; poster first, `preload="none"` on Save-Data.
- **Below the hero**: the Statement block is strong and should stay. The "Four steps" scroll-jack goes vertical on phones (11). The
  instrument picker gets three equal cards (12, 20). The product section gets fresh screenshots (27). The FAQ and final call to action
  are fine.
- **Nav**: plain text links with only "Get started" as a button (already proposed in `docs/PREMIUM-AUDIT.md` #10), server-rendered
  signed-in state (3), and the Discord icon joins the footer rather than sitting mid-bar.
- **Cookie banner** gone (8). On its own this is the biggest visual upgrade to the first screen on every device.

## F. Systems and operations

28. **`discord-class-channels` runs every minute and writes a `JobRun` row every time**: 1,440 rows and 1,440 Netlify function
    invocations a day (about 43,000 a month, a third of the free plan's 125,000). `JobRun` is 40,781 rows, never purged, and is the
    biggest table by far. Fix: run every 2 minutes, write a run row only when something happened or once per 15 minutes when idle
    (`runJob` option), and have the daily `credit-expiry` job delete `JobRun` rows older than 30 days (`atom-alert` rows excepted).
29. **Owner alert noise**: 29 "forwarded payment email was ignored" alerts in 30 days, 19 of them "signature check did not pass"
    (Gmail forwarding breaks the bank's signature; the app already knows this and says so). Fix: when Gmail recorded the bank's signature
    as passing, fold these into one daily digest line instead of one alert each, and send them to the owner only.
30. **Stale verification codes are never deleted**: 175 of 189 rows are expired. Add them to the daily purge.
31. **Flaky tests**: `tests/personalEmail.test.ts` and `tests/passwordChangedEmail.test.ts` time out at 5s under full-suite load (they
    pass alone in 0.7s). Import `@/lib/email` at the top of the file instead of inside each test, or set `testTimeout: 15_000` for
    those two.
32. **Branch `class-reschedule` is stale**: its three commits (Reschedule, Notify switch, notes) are already on main inside the #344
    batch, and main is 13 commits ahead. Restart from `origin/main` before any fix work (CLAUDE.md workflow rule 2).
33. **Two sessions share this working tree**: at the time of the audit another session had uncommitted changes deleting the Messages
    page and adding a `FRIEND_NUDGE` notification type, which broke the dev server mid-sweep. Fixes from this audit should go in a
    worktree of their own.
34. **Dependencies**: 5 moderate advisories, all through `@sentry/nextjs` → `@sentry/bundler-plugins` → `glob`/`minimatch` (build
    time only). `@sentry/nextjs` 11 and Prisma 7 are major updates and not urgent. `withSentryConfig` should be imported from
    `@sentry/nextjs/config` before v11.
35. **API routes that answer 500 with `console.error` only** (no `reportError`, so GlitchTip never sees them): assistant feedback,
    support, typing, suggestions, escalate; discord callback; register; lesson file; csp-report. Swap in `reportError`.
36. **`text-signal` dead class** still used in 7 places (`PricingManager.tsx`, `AdminAttendanceQuickPanel.tsx`, `NotificationBell.tsx`):
    that colour no longer exists, so "Changed" and the orange links render in the default colour. Use `text-signal-fg`.
37. **Security headers** are in good shape: strict CSP report-only with only one benign Turnstile report in production, HSTS,
    frame-ancestors none, COOP. The report-only policy has been clean for weeks; enforcing it is a one-line follow-up in
    `middleware.ts` (already noted in CLAUDE.md).
38. **Polling**: all timers honour hidden tabs except `ZelleUnlockGate` (8s while the pay sheet is open, acceptable) and the top
    banners' 15s check; fine.
